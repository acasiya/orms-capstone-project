    1. Who we are

    Barangay Platero, City of Biñan, Laguna ("the Barangay", "we") is the personal information controller for SafeSpace, the Barangay's Online Reporting and Management System. We follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

    We do not collect your precise location (GPS). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising or analytics tools.

    3. Our legal basis

    • Your consent (Sections 12(a) and 13(a)), given at sign-up, including for your voter's ID.
    • Functions of public authority (Section 12(e)): receiving complaints and enforcing barangay ordinances.
    • Compliance with a legal obligation (Section 12(c)), when a law or court order requires us to keep or disclose data.

    4. How we share your data

    We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We share it only:

    • Inside the Barangay: with officials and staff whose role needs it, as shown in the table above.
    • With service providers that run the system for us, under our instructions: [hosting provider] runs the application; [database provider] stores accounts, reports, concerns and logs; Cloudinary stores uploaded photos, videos, voter's IDs and profile pictures; and Brevo sends system emails such as verification codes and status updates.
    • With authorities when required by law, a subpoena or a court order, or to protect someone's life or safety.

    Some of these providers store data outside the Philippines. Under Section 21 of RA 10173, the Barangay stays accountable for your data and requires these providers to protect it at a comparable level.

    We do not reveal a reporter's identity to the person being reported, except when required by law or legal proceedings.

    6. How we protect it

    • All connections to the system are encrypted (HTTPS).
    • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
    • Access is limited by role: each official sees only what their position needs.
    • Accounts lock for 30 minutes after 10 failed login attempts.
    • Filing a report or resetting a password requires a one-time code sent to your email.
    • Login sessions expire automatically, and actions by officials are recorded in an audit log.
    • Officials with access are bound by confidentiality.

    7. Your rights

    Under Sections 16 to 18 of RA 10173, you have the right to:

    1. Be informed whether and how your personal data is being processed.
    2. Access your personal data and learn where it came from and who received it.
    3. Correct inaccurate or outdated data. You can edit most account details on your Profile page.
    4. Object to processing, or withdraw your consent. Some services, such as filing reports, cannot work without the data they need.
    5. Erasure or blocking of data that is inaccurate, unlawfully obtained or no longer needed.
    6. Data portability: receive a copy of your data in a common electronic format.
    7. Damages for harm caused by inaccurate or unlawfully used data.
    8. File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

    To use these rights, contact the Data Protection Officer. We will reply within [15] working days. We may ask you to prove your identity first.

    8. Data breaches

    If a breach is likely to put you at real risk of harm, we will notify the National Privacy Commission and affected users within 72 hours of learning of it, as required by NPC rules. The notice will say what happened, what data was involved, and what we are doing about it.

    9. Children

    Accounts are for registered voters of Barangay Platero, so users must be at least 18 years old.

    10. Changes to this policy

    We will post any changes here with a new effective date. For significant changes, we will notify you by email or when you next log in.
