    About Us

    Barangay Platero

    What is SafeSpace

    SafeSpace is a web-based system that is designed to help barangay citizens report violations, submit concerns and suggestions, ask questions, and view ordinances in order to promote collaborative and safe communities.

    Our Mission

    SafeSpace gives barangay citizens a way to check what happens to their reports, concerns and suggestions. It also helps promote local government accountability and action.
