    Ordinance Reporting and Management System

    What is O.R.M.S.?

    Ordinance Reporting and Management System, or O.R.M.S. for short, is a web-based system that is designed to help Barangay Citizens to report ordinance violations, as well as submit concerns and suggestions in order to promote collaborative and safe communities.

    Our Mission

    O.R.M.S gives the Barangay Citizen to check what happens to their reports and concerns and suggestions. It also helps promote Local Government Accountability and Action.
