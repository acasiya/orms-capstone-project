    Terms and Agreements

    Effective date: [date]

    By creating an account or using SafeSpace, the Barangay Platero Online Reporting and Management System, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, do not use the system. You can still report concerns in person at the Barangay Hall.

    1. Purpose of the system

    SafeSpace lets residents of Barangay Platero report violations of barangay ordinances, submit concerns and suggestions, ask questions, and view barangay ordinances. It lets barangay officials receive, investigate and respond to these submissions.

    SafeSpace is not an emergency service. For emergencies, call 911 or the barangay's emergency hotline [number].

    2. Who may use it

    • Residents: you must be at least 18 years old and a registered voter of Barangay Platero. Your account becomes active only after an Administrator verifies your voter's ID.
    • Barangay officials and staff: accounts are created by an Administrator. Access is limited to the duties of your position.

    3. Your account

    • Give true, complete and current information, and keep it up to date.
    • Keep your password private. Do not share your account or use someone else's.
    • You are responsible for everything done through your account. Tell the Barangay right away if you think someone else has used it.
    • One person may hold only one resident account.

    4. Filing reports and concerns

    • Report only incidents you believe in good faith actually happened. Describe them truthfully and accurately.
    • Attach only photos and videos you took or have the right to share, and that relate to the incident.
    • To prevent abuse, the system limits how often you can file reports and may ask you to confirm your identity with a code sent to your email.
    • Filing a report does not guarantee a particular action or outcome. The Barangay decides how to act according to law and its ordinances.
    • Your report and its attachments may be used as evidence in barangay proceedings, including Katarungang Pambarangay mediation, and may be shared with authorities when the law requires.

    5. What you must not do

    • File false, malicious or harassing reports. Knowingly false reports may lead to account suspension and liability under applicable laws, including laws on unjust vexation, perjury and libel.
    • Upload content that is obscene, violent, hateful or unrelated to the report, or that shows minors in a harmful way.
    • Post other people's personal information beyond what is needed to describe the incident.
    • Try to access data or features you are not authorized to use, disrupt the system, or bypass its security.
    • Use automated tools to create accounts or submit content.
    • Impersonate another person or a barangay official.

    6. Duties of barangay officials and staff

    Officials and staff with access to SafeSpace must:

    • Use personal data only for their official duties, and keep it confidential even after leaving office.
    • Not download, copy or share personal data outside the system except as their duties require.
    • Act on reports fairly and without bias, and record their actions honestly.
    • Report any suspected data breach to the Data Protection Officer immediately.

    All actions in the system are logged and may be reviewed.

    7. Suspension and termination

    The Barangay may suspend, disable or delete an account that breaks these Terms, gives false information, or is inactive for a long time. You may ask the Data Protection Officer to close your account at any time. Records we must keep by law may be retained as described in the Privacy Policy.

    8. Content you submit

    You keep ownership of what you submit. You allow the Barangay to use, store and share it only as needed to handle your submission and to perform its official functions, as described in the Privacy Policy.

    9. Availability and limits of liability

    We work to keep SafeSpace available and accurate, but it may be interrupted for maintenance or by technical problems. Ordinance texts in the system are for reference only; the official copies at the Barangay Hall prevail. To the extent allowed by law, the Barangay is not liable for losses caused by interruptions or by actions of users.

    10. Privacy

    Our Privacy Policy explains how we handle your personal data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), and forms part of these Terms.

    11. Changes to these Terms

    We may update these Terms. We will post the new version with its effective date and ask you to accept significant changes when you next log in.
